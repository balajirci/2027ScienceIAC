How to use the vaults

The colour-set packs (White, Red, and the sets still to come) are built to the tournament syllabus. The vaults are different: one trainer per science discipline, kept as depth stock. Use a vault to patch a weak discipline, to go deeper than the set bank reaches, or for finals-level stretch questions. Each vault trainer logs study time on this device, like the other study pages.
